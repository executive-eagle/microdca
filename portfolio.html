About this portfolio

On Oct 14, 2026 we pretended to put $10,000 into bitcoin and five income investments, buying at that day's closing prices. No real money is involved.

Every day, each income holding earns its yearly dividend ÷ 365 per share. That money is automatically used to buy more of the same holding at that day's price. More shares means a slightly bigger payout tomorrow. That's compounding. On days the market is closed, the last closing price is used.

Tap 1Y, 5Y or 10Y to watch a time-lapse of how dividends could stack up if every price stayed exactly where it is today. Real prices will move.

How this works

Same start, two paths. Both strategies start with the same bitcoin and the same extra savings, and both pay the same bills on the 1st of every month.

A, sell bitcoin: the extra savings buy more bitcoin on day one. Each month it sells the oldest bitcoin first, enough to cover the bill and the capital-gains tax on that sale.

B, live off income: the extra savings buy the income mix you pick, using real past payouts. Payouts pay the bills (after any tax). If they fall short, the gap is borrowed at your margin rate. Extra income pays the loan down first, then buys more income funds. Your bitcoin is never sold.

Simplified: ignores trading fees, assumes your broker lends against your holdings at the rate you choose, and doesn't model margin calls. Watch the "Loan vs. holdings" number. Above about 30–40%, a real broker may start selling for you. Read the full strategy, risks included

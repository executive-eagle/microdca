Sell bitcoin, or live off income?

Same starting money, two ways to pay your bills, replayed on real prices (including the 2026 crash).

Sell bitcoin: income pays the bills first, then bitcoin is sold for the rest (plus the tax). Keep bitcoin: bitcoin is never sold; income pays the bills, and any shortfall is borrowed.

How this works

Same start, two paths. Both plans start with the same money on the same day. Each splits it between bitcoin and the income mix you pick, and both pay the same bills on the 1st of every month, using real past prices and payouts.

Sell bitcoin: any income pays the bills first. Whatever's left is covered by selling the oldest bitcoin first, enough to pay the bill and the capital-gains tax on that sale.

Keep bitcoin: income pays the bills (after any tax). If it falls short, the gap is borrowed at your borrowing rate, and bitcoin is never sold. Leftover income pays the loan down first, then is split between buying more bitcoin and buying more income funds, however you choose. Selling handles its leftovers the same way.

Simplified: ignores trading fees, assumes your broker lends against your holdings at the rate you choose, and doesn't model margin calls. Watch the "Loan vs. holdings" number. Above about 30–40%, a real broker may start selling for you. Read the full strategy, risks included

DCA vs. lump sum

About DCA

Dollar cost averaging means buying a fixed dollar amount on a schedule (say $100 every week) no matter what the price is. When bitcoin is cheap, your $100 buys more. When it's expensive, it buys less. You never have to guess the right time to buy.

A lump sum means putting everything in on day one. It wins when prices mostly rise afterward, and it hurts more if you buy right before a drop.

About income investing

These investments pay you cash, called a dividend or distribution, just for holding them. Strategy's preferred stocks pay a set yearly rate on a $100 "stated amount". BTCI is an ETF that sells options on bitcoin funds to generate monthly payouts.

Reinvesting means each payout buys more shares, so the next payout is a little bigger. That snowball is called compounding. Money you spend or use to pay down debt stops compounding. "Opportunity cost" shows what that money could have grown into if it had been invested instead.

A high yield isn't free money. Preferred dividends can be suspended (STRD's are non-cumulative and wouldn't be paid back), and BTCI's price has fallen as it paid out. Borrowing on margin can force selling in a downturn. Always check total value, not just income.

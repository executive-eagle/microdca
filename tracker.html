How the estimate works

At-the-market (ATM) sales let a company sell new shares a little at a time on the open market. Strategy and Strive only sell these preferreds at or above their $100 par value, and they've said the money goes mainly to buying bitcoin.

The estimate: shares traded at or above $100 (5-minute data today, daily data before) × the share the company is assumed to sell × the price ÷ bitcoin's price at the time. On days a fund only touched $100 without closing there, half its volume counts.

The assumption: MicroDCA assumes the company sold about 45% of at-or-above-par volume. That roughly matches STRC growing to about $8.5 billion by spring 2026, about $6 billion beyond its first offering. Change it below to see a range.

Why buy a little at a time?

Buying a set dollar amount on a schedule (say $25 every week) is called dollar cost averaging, or DCA. When bitcoin is cheap, your $25 buys more. When it's expensive, it buys less. You never have to guess the "right" time to buy, and you never have to come up with a big lump of money.

Putting everything in on day one (a lump sum) does better when prices mostly rise afterward, and worse if you buy right before a drop. Nobody knows which one is coming.
